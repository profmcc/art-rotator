Ars Serena

Open-access museum art on your Lock Screen and desktop

Ars Serena keeps a rotating collection of public-domain and openly licensed artwork from museums around the world — the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the National Gallery of Art, SMK in Copenhagen, Japan Search and Wikimedia Commons — cropped to your screen and captioned with the title, artist and year.

Support Setup, troubleshooting, and how to reach a real person Privacy Policy What the app collects: nothing. In detail, and why that is structural

Ars Serena collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It downloads artwork and never uploads anything.
